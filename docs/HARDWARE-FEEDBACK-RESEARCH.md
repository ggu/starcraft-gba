# Protoss research follow-up

The custom-game Forge and Cybernetics Core report requires a shared research implementation, not a menu-only shortcut. At canonical source `31a5ddf8`, the fixed B-menu WEAPONS and ARMOR rows call `start_research` in `starcraft/game.c`; for Protoss that path requires any completed friendly Forge, charges 100 minerals and 100 gas, runs a 600-update timer, and caps each of two global levels at two. The selection of a Forge or Cybernetics Core does not expose the source building's separate upgrade choices. The current global weapon and armor fields are used by common actor/combat code and stored in the existing save state; they do not represent per-owner ground, air and shield upgrade families or the Cybernetics Core's research.

A complete change needs source-verified upgrade identities, prerequisites, price and duration per level, eligible producers, power and interruption/cancellation behavior, per-owner paid state, affected weapons/armor/shields and gameplay-visible feedback. It must define bounded persistence and validation for in-progress and completed research, including older journals and all campaign/custom owners, before admitting the new state. Normal-input Forge and Core tests need to verify unavailable choices, payment once, progress, power transitions, cancellation, completion and fresh-process save/load. ARM RAM, stack and timing and the all-product matrix remain required. Existing Protoss 10 custom feedback and source audit establish a gap, not a qualified implementation.
