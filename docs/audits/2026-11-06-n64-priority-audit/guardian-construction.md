## Ranked gaps and acceptance

1. **Guardian production cannot yet be safely activated across products.** Confidence high for current implementation, low for exact N64 runtime parity. `starcraft/game.c` retains explicit HOST-only Guardian, combined Egg and Scarab fences; `starcraft/sc64_guardian_morph_adapter.h` has a real paid in-place HOST lifecycle and selected controls, but no qualified combined guest journal, ordinary campaign constructor path, art or SRAM. The HOST conversion's 750-update clock and proportional HP are PC-derived approximations. Z8–Z10 independent paid Egg guest continuations exist only for the stated opt-in profiles and four cases (`../2026-11-06-current-native-qualification/late-eggs.md`); they do not establish a Guardian or N64 timing result. **Scope:** integrate a shared owner/PUNI-aware Guardian service, candidate-local selected feature context, legacy and combined Egg/Scarab wire translations, capacity and identity checks. **Accept:** real ordinary-input paid Mutalisk→Cocoon→Guardian and damaged cancel/refund on each applicable guest profile, fresh-process genuine SRAM at mid-morph and after birth, predecessor and bad-newer fallback, old raw-36 Overlord, paired Eggs/trigger/Carrier boundaries, plus RAM/stack and crowded ARM timing. Capture an untouched N64 morph and cancel, recording resources, owner, HP, displayed frame/update timing and both animations before claiming parity.

2. **Campaign prerequisites and paid cancellation need an authenticated save contract.** Confidence high for prototype behavior and missing protection; exact N64 morph accounting remains unobserved. Local experimental `starcraft/sc64_guardian_campaign_construction.h` adds Queens Nest and in-place Hive/Greater Spire with source PUNI checks for Z5–Z10, rejects earlier unsupported PUNI contexts, preserves larvae/timers and real paid Egg, and is fenced to HOST. `tests/sc64_guardian_campaign_construction_game.c` passes constructed Z7 HOST checks at `4380a750`; a compact building leaf codec retains the staged row. It does **not** prove selected-journal safety or guest save/restore. The provisional building flag `0x2000` is not reserved: `starcraft/race_campaign_adapter.h` copies CHK `src->flags` into building flags, and old tags do not authenticate this marker. Interpreting it as proof of payment could refund an imported or legacy row. Also, current Guardian start uses the old broad `race_zerg_campaign_product_available` predicate rather than this new slot-specific PUNI route. **Scope:** audit every building codec/source flag and all historical tag combinations; authenticate paid morph provenance in an allocated selected-candidate namespace, reject malformed or old-context marker rows, and generate trusted early Z1–Z4 PUNI mappings. **Accept:** actual source-owner admission and nonspending denials, interrupted and captured builders, incomplete prereqs, damaged morph cancellation/75% PC-proxy refund until N64-verified, existing Egg/larva and HP/remainder preservation, and old/new journal round trips with atomic rejection and fresh-process guest SRAM.

3. **Construction completion and damage presentation remain broad, measurable gaps.** Confidence high in the implementation gap; native exact timing and pixels unproven. `starcraft/game.c` uses source construction art only while `under_construction`, then shows the completed building; the source bank contains a Warp Anchor and modifier/underlay metadata, but late Protoss materialization and Assimilator geyser attachment remain unfinished (`../2026-10-26-construction-lifecycle/README.md`, `../2026-10-26-construction-modifiers/README.md`). `docs/BUILDING-DAMAGE.md` describes implemented shared fire/damage overlays and a PC-derived Terran burn rule; blending, random attachment history, alternate-pose anchors and the native burn handler are unverified. Shield absorption currently lacks a directional target impact (`docs/ENGINE-FIDELITY-AUDIT.md`). **Scope:** obtain one bounded ordinary N64 Pylon and Assimilator completion plus shielded-hit and damaged-building comparison; map source image/IScript and destination-dependent blending before implementation. **Accept:** equivalent frame-by-frame transition, attachment, location, palette and clipping; correct HP/shield changes and save/reload during transitions, with bounded transient capacity and all-product tests. Do not infer N64 timing or exact blending from OpenBW alone.

4. **Production art/facts need a complete guest-specific bank and combined roster.** Confidence high in current scope, low in N64 animation timing. Cocoon source image 11/12 and its source Init frames are documented in `../2026-11-05-guardian-morph/README.md`; current renderer and bank are HOST-only. Egg static guest HUD evidence does not establish birth/pulse/shadow timing. Local commit `7e280ab2` generated a separate ignored full Guardian/Egg P10 fact table and compared every staged actor row with the compiled reference under HOST sanitizers; this is a narrow current-roster fact test, not a guest build, art comparison, or the proposed Scarab-inclusive roster. A separate distinct-Guardian facts target was committed at `d9d549fa` but **not run**. **Scope:** regenerate complete, fingerprinted target-specific facts and Cocoon art in new private paths after the final append-only combined roster is fixed, with no modified pinned headers. **Accept:** row/enum/raw mapping and exact expected fact counts for every product, clipped/fogged actual ARM sprite and shadow checks, N64 capture comparison, linker reserve and stack measurement, and no proprietary files in Git.

No ordinary Z8/Z10 win, global 30 fps claim, full save compatibility or complete N64 equivalence follows from this audit.
