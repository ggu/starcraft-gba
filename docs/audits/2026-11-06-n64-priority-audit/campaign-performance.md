## Highest-impact gaps and scoped acceptance

1. **Shared production, persistence and completion (high confidence in product gaps; limited N64 behavioral evidence).** Main retains the paid Egg services as previews, not normal activation. Draft #298's six normal-product builds reproduce previously qualified preview bytes, and four actual late-campaign Egg save/birth continuations and six custom HOST input fixtures are evidence within their specific contexts; they are not all-campaign completion. Its Z8 controller still loses with the candidate and service-off trajectories differing; this is a release gate, not proof of an Egg regression. Z10 lacks an ordinary win. Existing paid-Reaver/Scarab and Guardian features remain incomplete or staged. See [common release audit](../2026-11-06-common-egg-release/README.md), [late Egg proof](../2026-11-06-current-native-qualification/late-eggs.md), `starcraft/sc64_engine_capabilities.h`, and `starcraft/sc64_zerg_late_ai.h`.

   Require ordinary-input Z8 resolution without objective/resource/enemy changes, Z10's actual Temple/crystal/result and genuine SRAM continuation, and carried-mission regression. Exercise Egg/Guardian/Scarab/Carrier capacity, ownership, save/journal migration, slot reuse and cancellation across the actual production vectors. Bound actor and memory use and retain normal linker/stack guards. Source-script rows and HOST controllers do not establish N64 wave execution, paid ammunition timing, native unit-cap behavior or a native win. Obtain untouched native production, arrival/target and handoff observations before claiming parity.

2. **Custom games and fog (high confidence in limited coverage; native details uncertain).** Both maps and race/AI combinations have extensive startup/save coverage, but that is not a complete 1–3-AI progression or match win. Blood Bath N64 captures support 4/10 Terran and 4/9 Zerg/Protoss observed supply; the later GBA opening adjustment targets this bounded discrepancy, not a fully observed native base inventory, initial wallet or AI schedule. See [opening adjustment](../2026-11-05-skirmish-opening-supply/README.md) and `starcraft/race_adapter.h`. Run both maps with each human race, mixed 1–3 AI choices, real economy and tech progression, victory, crowded actor capacity and actual fresh-process save continuation. Compare controlled native starting resources, visible buildings, mining/production and AI launches before changing balance.

   Fog is functional but not established as N64-equivalent. Human auto-acquisition uses current center-cell visibility; AI has a separate model. Source sight facts and incremental visibility oracles are evidence about the implementation and pinned PC-derived facts, not native parity. See `starcraft/sc64_visibility.h`, [human auto-acquisition](../2026-10-29-auto-acquisition-fog/README.md), and [human source sight](../2026-10-29-human-source-sight/README.md). Capture untouched N64 reveal/explored persistence, moving targets across sight edges, detector/cloak, selection and attacks with independently identified units. Compare gameplay state and RGB output over camera/terrain changes, save/load, structures completing/dying and mixed AI; qualify both maps and busy timing before enabling a fog-rendering alternative. The held fog work is not a measured performance fix.

3. **Busy-frame cost and route tail (high confidence in the scoped emulator measurements, not physical hardware).** The exact pre-activation P7 normal-vector replacement loop costs 2,200,225.76 cycles per advancing update over 958 active updates, with three modal acknowledgements and 90 sampled state/pixel checks. A 30 fps GBA period is about 559,000 cycles, so approximately 74.6% of this sampled loop would have to disappear to fit that period, ignoring scheduling/presentation complications. The phase build adds 3.32% and reports inclusive, nested scopes: order setup 582,673, Arbiter 457,829, movement 367,982 and full render 602,805 cycles/update. They cannot be summed as independent savings. Source/evidence: [current qualification](../2026-11-06-current-native-qualification/README.md), `/tmp/sc64-current-perf-result-nov6/verified.json`; `starcraft/game.c`, `starcraft/sc64_routes.h`, and `starcraft/sc64_contact.h`.

   New route diagnostic `199a2912` measured the same pre-activation workload, matching 90 checks and all workload counts with 0.133% loop overhead. At update 8347 one `(420,108) → (552,1249)` route call spent 2,264,933 cycles building a full reverse flood with 12,001 enqueued cells; zero blocked-start gradient steps. The other 11 captured calls totaled 59,714 cycles, with no drops. This is a specific synchronous tail, not proof of a persistent mean bottleneck: amortizing that entire call over 958 updates is about 2,364 cycles/update. Evidence `/tmp/sc64-route-tail-199a2912-result/verified.json`. An exact kernel optimization may reduce the peak, but preserve complete field bytes, queue/tie order, borrowed-grid restoration, invalidation and same-update movement; test against `tests/race_routes.c` and `tests/sc64_route_subpath.c`, then compare normal-vector mean and tail with full per-update state and sampled pixels. No delayed movement or skipped actor ticks.

   Earlier movement diagnostics measured roughly 331k cycles/update in collision/steering, with 654,824 initial bucket visits and 731,178 mask visits; these intrusive historical figures identify a larger mean opportunity, not guaranteed current savings. A prior reuse candidate improved late timing by 1.17% but regressed an early window by 3.03%. The detector-first candidate also regressed the exact current late pair by 0.107%, so neither is a release recommendation. Any new algorithm needs collision-mask and first-step scalar oracles, matching full per-update state, early/late P7/P10 and 1-/3-AI runs, and memory/stack checks. Establish a meaningful full-loop improvement and p95/p99/worst-update results under representative scenes, then verify on physical hardware; no hardware 30 fps claim follows from these replacement-loop samples.

4. **Native timing research (high confidence in recorded observation; low confidence in generalization).** The [same-process interpreter trace](../2026-11-05-n64-order-cadence/README.md) has four identified worker timers decrementing once in each of 126 complete countdown intervals. It does not prove a global world clock, every order's cadence, JIT behavior, weapon timing, or a nine-update whole-actor skip. A pause-correlated candidate was retracted as an RNG-like value. Reobserve selected movement/stop, attack, cooldown, death/transport and speed/pause transitions in independently validated actors; correlate writer events with an independently established active world clock and visible outcomes. Change timing only after such evidence and exact regression checks.

## Evidence boundaries and priorities

The independent priority remains shared paid lifecycles and compatible persistence first, while measurable movement/collision work proceeds in parallel; then ordinary campaign/custom completion and broader projectile parity. The current P10 early-save matrix adds 21 old/new/migration finite neutral continuations to frame 1,031, not arbitrary saves or wins. All 21 retained `/tmp/sc64-p10-long-{old,new,legacy}-p1..p7/result.json` records report success and unchanged SRAM/inputs. The existing 29/30 delivered-content count is not a proof that all 29 win on the draft candidate: its controller record presently covers 28. These limits do not imply native game behavior from extracted rows or PC source, nor equivalence of host, GBA ARM and N64 execution.
