# N64 gas pickup and deposit

The authenticated USA ROM's resource code gives a **base pickup of eight gas**
when a refinery has at least eight remaining. Below eight, including zero,
it clears the remaining reserve and gives a base pickup of two. A conditional
AI-associated modifier can add one to the worker's cargo. The instruction path and the ordinary Blood Bath continuation below
independently support the depleted base amount. A successful return
to a depot is still required before the cargo reaches the bank.

Run `python3 tools/sc64_native_gas_audit.py --rom /path/to/StarCraft64-USA.z64`
to authenticate the normalized big-endian USA ROM and the four instruction
regions below. The check emits only digests and the interpreted facts. It
never writes or exports ROM bytes. The asset-free check is
`python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -p 'test_sc64_native_gas_audit.py'`.

## Instruction path

Addresses are native virtual addresses. The resource dispatcher at
`0x80133358` recognizes raw 110 (Refinery), 149 (Extractor), and 157
(Assimilator), and calls `0x801332ac` with resource kind zero. Raw 176–178
mineral fields reach the same helper with kind one. The helper reads an
unsigned 16-bit reserve from resource actor offset `0xcc`.

For a reserve of at least eight, the branch at `0x801332c8` falls through,
subtracts eight, stores the new reserve and returns eight. For a reserve
below eight, the branch reaches `0x8013332c`; kind zero takes the gas path,
which returns two and clears the reserve, even if the prior value was one
through seven. The mineral path is different and is not a gas oracle. The
return's delay slot at `0x80133338` performs the clear; it is not skipped.

The dispatcher checks `0x8004d1b8` and, when it returns nonzero, adds one to
the pickup before calling `0x80136b40`. That helper stores the amount in the
worker's byte at `0xcb`. The modifier requires a nonzero worker AI pointer at
`0x124`, owner record byte `0x37` equal to two, and the result of another
helper at least twelve. Its full policy and behavior in every custom-game
setting are not yet established. Three retained ordinary mixed Blood Bath
states at updates 19,444, 26,877 and 31,138 have owner-record byte one for
all four seats, so this modifier's byte-equals-two branch cannot be taken by
those seats at those cuts. This is not a claim about every game or future cut.

At `0x80136d74`, the deposit path distinguishes the cargo flags. Its gas
branch at `0x80136df4` through `0x80136e1c` adds the worker's cargo byte to
`0x800b1d74 + 4 * owner`, then clears the cargo. Thus eight and two are base
pickup amounts that become bank credits after return, subject to the modifier
and the deposit path actually completing.

## Independent observations and limits

The [gas-depletion TODO](../PROJECT-TODO.md) records a fresh Blood Bath HUD
showing 10,000 gas on a selected geyser; the source CHK stores 10,000 in its
32-bit site quantity. The live actor's `0xcc` reserve is unsigned 16-bit;
this observation does not establish how the N64 handles every possible CHK
quantity above 65,535 or prove that every map starts at 10,000.

Three prior retained ares states from the ordinary mixed Blood Bath run
provide coarse independent stock-reserve cuts. At updates 19,444, 26,877 and
31,138, Refinery actor slot 23 (raw 110, owner 2) had 7,160, 6,504 and 6,024
remaining. The differences are 656 and 480, multiples of eight. Extractor
slot 36 (raw 149, owner 0) held 7,272, 5,664 and 4,744; Assimilator slot 21
(raw 157, owner 3) held 8,160, 7,208 and 6,552. These samples are thousands
of updates apart, so they do not isolate a single pickup, deposit, or depleted
case. No emulator input or save-state mutation was used for this inspection.

The earlier private `ai-parity-20261119/sampler.py` read `0x801103d4` and
`0x80110404` as mineral and gas banks. Those addresses are not the deposit
arrays identified above. For example, at update 19,444 the old gas read was
all zero, whereas the actual four owner gas words at `0x800b1d74` were
20, 0, 582 and 626. The private sampler and its JSONL are not tracked; their
bank labels must not be used as wallet evidence. The original audit already
stated that computer banks had not been resolved. The stored states are too
far apart to prove a single gas payment.

The normalized ROM SHA-256 is
`e7d21880c13953b45b8f8db6b2b6cfa02daa29d35b23799a967e96f21001b020`.
The 444-byte pickup/dispatch region at ROM offset `0xf6f9c` has SHA-256
`5f699d01046af17310a2196658a943e581d13531bba3d723f6c531fdcaa9aca7`;
the same bytes appear in each of the three retained states. The audit tool
also authenticates the cargo, deposit and modifier regions. The result is
bounded to this USA ROM and these paths.

## GBA implementation checks

The opt-in build uses the same source site for placement, rendering and
resource accounting. Its save format stores site consumption independently
of building lifetimes. Blood Bath uses 10,000 gas per imported site. Outpost
uses an authored 5,000. Existing saves initialize site consumption to zero.

- **Campaign construction.** The three product HOST builds checked all 30
  source maps for distinct site coordinates and representable capacities.
  In Terran 3, Zerg 2 and Protoss 2, a preplaced refinery occupied a site
  absent from the neutral-node list. The fixture destroyed the structure,
  paid to rebuild it, advanced construction, delivered gas and reloaded
  the save. It seeds the wallet and positions workers at the site and depot.
  In each case the cartridge then loaded the HOST-produced mission save,
  continued, wrote a new pause save and restored its frame and site balances
  in a fresh mGBA process. Run `make check-sc64-source-gas` with the prepared
  private inputs to repeat the HOST checks.
- **Actual ARM pickup and deposit.** Each race cartridge loaded a Blood Bath
  fixture with eight gas remaining at its closest site, its worker ready to
  gather and the human gas bank empty. With no further button input, the SCV,
  Drone and Probe each carried eight at frame 1, deposited eight by frame 49,
  and carried two at frame 145. The bank reached ten by frame 181 for Zerg
  and 187 for Terran and Protoss. The site remained exhausted. The fixture
  seeds depletion and worker position; pickup, travel and deposit run on ARM.
- **Save migration.** A Terran 9 legacy pause save accrued 128 gas consumed at
  one site over 512 cartridge updates. A fresh process retained that balance.
  The older cartridge refused the depleted record when isolated and fell
  back to its older compatible slot when both were present. On all four
  cartridges a legacy three-AI save for each map also loaded with zero
  consumption, accrued site debt and restored its exact frame and balances
  after a pause save.
- **Chapter transitions.** On the field-geometry build, all 30 retained
  victory saves opened with the expected mission and completion bits. Each
  advanced into the next mission (or restarted the finale), wrote a pause
  save, and loaded at the expected frame in a fresh process with identical
  SRAM. These checks start from existing victories.
- **Final missions.** The finite-gas Terran cartridge won T10 at frame 3,361
  with a mid-mission save/load. The Protoss cartridge advanced from the P9
  result to P10, matched 22 controller checkpoints and won at frame 53,225.
  Both victory journals restored in fresh processes. With the feature off,
  all four products' linked loadable bytes matched the release controls.
- **Custom-game setup.** All 72 combinations of cartridge, map, human race
  and opponent count opened using controller inputs. Each case checked the
  active seat mask, its chosen CPU races and 12 simulation updates. The three
  chosen seat layouts per human race include closed seats and mixed CPU races.
- **Custom-game performance.** Eight paired runs each continued the same
  legacy three-AI save for 2,048 updates. At 64 samples per run, semantic
  world summaries, actor arrays, mineral arrays, both video pages and the
  palette matched release. On Showcase Outpost the display selected the
  opposite page at one sample and the two page images differed. Aggregate
  cycle differences stayed under 0.05%. Timings below are mGBA emulated
  CPU cycles converted to simulation updates per second.

## Campaign qualification with finite reserves enabled

The canonical build recipes for Terran, Zerg, Protoss and Showcase produced
the same four ROM hashes as the measured opt-in builds. On Zerg 7 the original
HOME input sequence won at frame 267,585 and the resulting SRAM re-exported
byte for byte from a fresh emulator. The home site started with 4,250 gas
and was exhausted. The player finished with 6,646 gas and zero enemy
buildings. Of the named campaign fields, only gas changed across the 206
recorded checkpoints relative to the earlier unlimited economy.

From the preceding Zerg 9 victory, the corrected Zealot-era controller also
won Zerg 10 at frame 198,954. Across 97 checkpoints the only changed field
was the resource bank; the final human gas balance was 13,445, compared
with 57,305 on the unlimited reference. The mission recorded no
unsupported events, retained all ten chapter flags and re-exported identical
victory SRAM after fresh load. The older pre-Zealot input sequence had
already diverged on the release control before this economic change.

## Gas service at one building

Three neutral-input ares continuations on the USA ROM sampled the workers
after every active video field. Each run covered 1,800 fields plus its initial
state. The observer read the simulation counter, selected workers' orders,
cargo and positions, site reserve and owner gas bank. The two stocked-site
runs also read each worker's order-target pointer. The cuts come from one
ordinary multiplayer Blood Bath session with computer opponents.

| Native starting frame | Workers tracked | Observed service |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 38,433 | Four Drones and one SCV | Three Drones completed eight empty-cargo order-83 intervals targeting Extractor slot 36. The SCV completed one at Refinery slot 23. Every complete interval spanned 37 simulation-counter increments from first to last sample. |
| 62,539 | Three Probes | Twelve complete intervals targeted Assimilator slot 21. The spans were 36–37 counter increments. In twelve successive handoffs the Probes repeatedly occupied that same site. |
| 68,224 | Two Probes | The reserve fell from 32 to zero. Complete intervals spanned 36–37 increments on both sides of depletion. Cargo changed from eight to two. |

At every sampled field, at most one *tracked* worker at a given site had
order 83. Different sites could serve workers concurrently. For example,
the Drone and SCV intervals around simulation frame 38,550 overlap at two
different buildings. The first and last sampled counter values can coincide
across a handoff because a VI boundary can fall inside a simulation update.
These observations measure the occupied interval separately from approach,
return and deposit. They leave the arbitration rule for simultaneous arrivals
and worker loss open.

On the packaged GBA Protoss10 ROM (SHA-256 prefix `19d1cfb3`), load the
retained three-computer Blood Bath game at frame 10,257, then sample 400
successive simulation frames with neutral buttons. Count empty workers with
`ORDER_GAS`, the same building target and a positive `work_timer`. At least
two workers counted down concurrently in 112 frames at site slot 9, 177 at
slot 15 and 216 at slot 25. Their respective maxima were two, three and
three. The shared GBA gather code initializes a separate 60-update gas timer
per worker. The save SHA-256 is
`7170785abc3f450e4eb02eb428ccc9f216f2d7d1b00f4b1fd242267008ddf8d7`.

Qualify single occupancy independently of the timer value. A native
simulation increment and a GBA update represent different amounts of play
time. An implementation needs a saturated site, independent adjacent sites,
worker cancellation or death, building destruction and a fresh SRAM reload
while another worker waits. Recheck paid production and full matches before
changing the shared economy.
