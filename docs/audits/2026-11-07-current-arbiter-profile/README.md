# Current-source P10 combat profiling

At production source `8e9f0a0a`, the normal P10 control and a separate nested-diagnostic build used the current qualified warp-audio bank and the same ordinary physical-button P7 campaign tape. The control ROM SHA-256 is `eae4a4e97ef50fafeff8038a68f675cd51eb88f2a456c5e159b445ccba88ad6f`; the diagnostic ROM is `12983e6c15edc9a40dd8f833e9fc3ef1b8094c5ec19c89e81b1bb71e2a569af8`. Both preserved the normal linker guards; control/diagnostic P10 margins were respectively 19,992/19,936 EWRAM bytes and 2,056/1,888 IWRAM bytes. The 595 copied inputs and controller files remained hash-identical under the shared lock. Private exact vectors, inputs, logs and results are under `.local/perf-current/` and `.local/qualification/p10-busy/`.

The matched 1,000-call combat window contains 958 advancing calls, 42 nonadvancing calls and three ordinary modal acknowledgments. Frame/screen/mode timelines matched, as did 11 sampled selected-world-state/fog hashes and complete displayed indexed framebuffer/palette hashes. This is not every-frame equality, a full campaign result, or a hardware FPS measurement. Uninstrumented mean advancing loop cost was 2,372,998 emulated cycles; diagnostic mean was 2,428,966, a 2.359% observer overhead.

Diagnostic nested means were 667,526 cycles in order setup, 151,884 in Arbiter aura work, and 390,148 in Arbiter cache updates, of which detector refresh consumed 207,680. These scopes overlap and cannot be added as independent savings. Movement averaged 357,258 cycles and dominates the sampled worst order spike; mean and tail have different causes. The prior rejected contact-mask prototype is not enabled. This is attribution, not an optimization or an assertion that these costs can all be removed.

An opt-in 16-entry same-tick optical-cell memo at `e2f5b585` was also rejected. It reused only cell-centered optical visibility; detector geometry remained per target and the no-list scalar path was unchanged. The scalar owner oracle, same-cell/different-target and snapshot-rebuild cases, P10/Z9 sight fixtures, and unchanged P10 ARM guards passed. The ordinary-input pair matched timeline/modal counts and all 11 sampled state/fog/displayed framebuffer/palette hashes. Nevertheless mean cost rose from 2,372,998 to 2,380,870 cycles (+0.332%). A separate diagnostic-only classification at `c37f5050` retained those samples and counted 345 hits, 3,538 first-seen misses, 64 conflict misses and no unclassified misses across the 958 advancing updates. The small conflict count does not justify a hash sweep. Logs and receipts are `.local/perf-current/memo-pair-results.json` and `miss-results.json`. This experiment remains default-off and is not a speed gain.
