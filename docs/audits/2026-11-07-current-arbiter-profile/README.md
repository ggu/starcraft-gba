Frame/screen/mode timelines matched, as did 11 sampled selected-world-state/fog hashes and complete displayed indexed framebuffer/palette hashes. This is not every-frame equality, a full campaign result, or a hardware FPS measurement. Diagnostic nested means were 667,526 cycles in order setup, 151,884 in Arbiter aura work, and 390,148 in Arbiter cache updates, of which detector refresh consumed 207,680. These scopes overlap and cannot be added as independent savings. Movement averaged 357,258 cycles and dominates the sampled worst order spike; mean and tail have different causes. The prior rejected contact-mask prototype is not enabled. This is attribution, not an optimization or an assertion that these costs can all be removed.

Advancing mean fell from 2,372,998 to 2,337,591 cycles (1.492%, about 35,407 cycles); sampled maximum fell from 3,959,806 to 3,956,497. This is one bounded emulator window, not complete state/pixel proof, hardware FPS, or a material solution to the 30-fps gap.
