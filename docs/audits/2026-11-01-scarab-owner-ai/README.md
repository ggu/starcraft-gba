`race_scarab_ai_enqueue_one` checks the live parent and the caller's owner and source-owner snapshot, refuses the human owner, and delegates one paid queue entry to the staged Scarab model. Imported custom opponents use their independent engine-owner mineral banks. P7 enemy source owners 0 and 4 and P8 enemy source owners 0 and 3 use their respective source wallets. The seeded fixture covers owner mismatches, unsupported sources, payment and capacity, and a custom-map setup matrix; it does not prove natural Reaver production or gameplay.

Possible integrations must use existing owner decisions rather than adding an unqualified native-AI claim. Custom skirmish currently plans each registered bot every 48 engine frames. P8 town decisions use 120 updates, with two towns sharing source owner 3, so a caller must avoid charging that owner twice. Z8 town production decisions use 240 updates and have separate source owners. These are approximate existing game policies, not native AI cadence. P7's authored RunAIScript replacement is not a recurring economic scheduler, and Showcase has no equivalent recurring economy planner. Their caller and typed owner authority remain to be designed and qualified.

The service currently forces the ordinary unupgraded capacity of five. The pinned [BWAPI upgrade data](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bwapi/bwapi/d727fed68558c506163048ea889131d8cbb33915/bwapi/BWAPILIB/Source/UpgradeType.cpp) specifies Reaver Capacity at 200 minerals, 200 gas, 2500 source ticks and one level, researched at a Robotics Support Bay. Pinned [OpenBW source](https://github.com/OpenBW/openbw/blob/4b046d5f65302b10cb0a745f0fecd37ec85b20a8/bwgame.h) describes ordinary capacity five or ten and hero Reaver capacity ten; the current raw-83 parent service does not cover that hero. These references establish source facts, not Nintendo 64 equivalence.

A complete implementation still needs per-owner research permissions and initial state, independent paid jobs and cancellation, source-clock and pause behavior, atomic persistence and load validation, upgraded/hero capacity, natural production and AI decisions, projectile lifecycle and combat, artwork, memory and performance qualification. Campaign source permissions must be represented separately from shared mechanics. Do not reuse unrelated transport job bytes or campaign persistence as a general owner store. No full AI, combat, save, cross-mode, or hardware-performance parity is claimed.
