# Experimental Arbiter aura bounds and source hoists

Two default-off probes preserve existing aura traversal and source/current-owner rules. `SC64_AURA_SQUARE_HOIST_PROBE` computes each eligible Arbiter's clipped finder-square bounds once for that Arbiter instead of once per target. `SC64_AURA_SOURCE_HOIST_PROBE` reuses the source-to-live-owner binding already authenticated by the outer Arbiter loop when testing targets. Neither changes actor limits, clock cadence, target order or save state.

The combined probes passed the independent scalar boundary/random square oracle, actual P7 cache geometry/cadence policy fixture, and an actual-engine 80-world randomized aura differential with probes off and both on, under ASan/UBSan. Control and candidate output hashes were identical for policy (`341182cf203068d618b6f0d3d81461e772aec96aa86d8c3f7a7e7720b6ecb4d5`) and aura oracle (`34d505037756f0a313f73d76852e2e9bcf1fe324501470447a7fcc479d9f7fec`). The square oracle log was `2b0cc13e5efb0adfb8e2756490f0bdd699dd0d756bb08c5f2dc908d49239b2a9`. Source reviews identified no actionable defect in the bounded hoists.

For frozen source `388ec497d75a17c2521ce2e087405060ffdf335d`, a P10 ordinary P7-input replay matched all 1,000 frame/mode transitions and 11 sampled selected-world, full displayed indexed framebuffer and palette checks: 958 advancing and 42 nonadvancing calls with three modal acknowledgments. All samples contained three raw-type-71 Arbiters. Mean cycles were 2,306,990.83 control and 2,265,378.15 combined (1.8038% fewer); maxima were 3,956,748 and 3,954,788. Both guarded links left 19,992 bytes EWRAM and 2,056 bytes IWRAM. Candidate ROM SHA-256 was `70e9800f7d9803d8a70a05a417b1b7939650999800010efdd0153ebc1ade0415`.

The control replay was the earlier independently qualified bounds-only test, not rerun for this combined source. Reuse required the new OFF ROM to match the complete normal 32 MiB ROM `317cc67d8f48b6771b3ebf4497c52b290466de49d097133b7b1bfdea65b34cd9`, full ELF symbol/address/size maps, Unit, Building and compact-building DWARF layouts, frontend offsets, and the prior observer, reader, tape and control receipt hashes. Both probes remained off in that control. The combined pair receipt SHA-256 is `ac6b90f4b4ad43a09bfaf23f46a26d2bfbb60c22fa5bb702489383fe500682d0`; the 595-input manifest was `47f5ac9fd8d74ef29e22c2296109c4f2ef2ea46e493f26c6cb9598058018e6ee`, and inputs were unchanged.

This is one nonterminal emulator combat window with sampled pixels and state, not every-frame parity, hardware FPS, a full campaign or near-30-fps proof. No normal product enables these flags; broader modes, ordinary play, save and all-product resource guards remain open. The standalone bounds probe independently measured 0.4333% in this scene; its earlier pre-guest observer-permission failure is retained in that separate audit. Private ROMs, assets and run records remain ignored.
