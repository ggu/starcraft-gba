# Z8 corrected-clock controller experiments

These local host experiments start from experimental source `e1240d93a088e03a0561b3fc17e822b9e7efcdbb`, which includes the nominal four-native-steps-per-five-engine-updates Z8 correction. The controllers inspect live state to plan and issue gameplay actions through normal button inputs. They are oracle-assisted policies, not demonstrations of ordinary human play. They do not modify mission objectives, actor stats, AI, resource balances or world state directly. No save/load was used.

The existing explicit-Move policy from `c2bf93d` lost at engine frame 2407, scheduler frame 1925, with no scheduler fault. The final report recorded a Dark Templar in the third hive's source location; this supports but does not by itself identify the exact defeat trigger. The prior pre-clock version had reached frame 10822, so the old result cannot qualify the corrected clock. The unchanged-strategy log SHA-256 is `81795fb3b04deb4577c39c15d4ec10195c149deea82b231d546cc2335c843ced`.

A policy that issued the same southern Overlord and defense commands before the northern commands survived the first two raids and lost at engine frame 13528, native frame 10822. The live source-condition census for T17 returned one matching Dark Templar in location 18 at defeat. The original southern detector was alive at frame 12600 and dead by 12840, and nearby southern defenders were also lost. This census proves the condition was true at defeat, not a unique internal firing trace. Log SHA-256: `885cce5e2d71c7747cf1adbcc6f21f63e1ff4f754220fea378ddc3724ac3f1f2`.

The next policy paid to train one replacement Overlord at frame 8373 and sent it directly south. It was damaged by nearby hostile units and died near the northern base at frame 10701, before reaching the southern defense. The sampled proximity, cooldown and target fields do not identify a unique attacker. The run lost at engine frame 13528; log SHA-256: `cf1f5204f81b2d635629c6117dc6013d68dad4ff1693500596cc345f1a7f8091`. The reported wallet change across menu actions includes concurrent mining and is not itself the unit price; the source rule charges 100 minerals.

An earlier-paid, multi-waypoint flight kept its replacement Overlord alive but did not position it in time. It was admitted at frame 7425, received its first waypoint at frame 8249, and reached the next waypoint before the second Move was issued at frame 9798. At defeat, engine frame 13450/native frame 10760, it was still far north of the hive entrance. Log SHA-256: `e4f1aa0570624e06805169b30ff5fd6de6da8e7017a04e70913606b0f7db6e8a`.

All four host runs used the canonical Z8 host compiler vector, ASan/UBSan, and the exclusive native lock. The 109 pinned inputs and five explicitly identified sidecars, including the complete Nydus bank and Z8 program, were checked before and after each run. The logs and generated inputs remain private and are not included in this branch.

The existing-detector/ground-route policy in `tests/sc64_z8_existing_detector_probe.c` was run once on docs-only head `4694771`. It verified the western Overlord's live identity and issued its first two waypoint moves, but lost to the first raid at engine frame 2407/native frame 1925. T17's live location-18 census was one at frame 2400 and at defeat; the Dark Templar remained hidden while the southern detector was farther north. The later Hydralisk route was never reached. The 109 pinned inputs and five sidecars were verified before and after; log SHA-256: `c30e600eb26d5c80e67ab1fc5b1051627097b97dd30f681c60930b9d08b79158`. The additional early command and altered timing correlate with a different first-raid path; the log does not establish the cause.

No source or binary result from this branch demonstrates a Z8 mission win, native AI parity, source-hardware timing, or near-30 performance.
