# Showcase reusable HUD bands and minimap

The Showcase product now uses the existing page-band HUD cache and minimap raster already selected by the three canonical race products. `SHOWCASE_HUD_CACHE=0` selects the prior EWRAM-band implementation; invalid values are rejected and the configuration key forces consumers to rebuild on a mode change. The Terran, Zerg and Protoss expanded flags are unchanged. The unchanged static terrain and game rules, actor counts, audio and render cadence remain in use.

Showcase scene labels overwrite the bottom HUD band after it is drawn. The page-band path now invalidates that page after a label. A poisoned-page pixel oracle with a stable underlying HUD key exposed a stale label without the hook at pixel (181,147); it passed with the hook. The ordinary `sc64_showcase_leave` path returns through a title clear, so this is a defensive out-of-band scene transition, not a claim that the ordinary leave path was visibly broken. An initial negative-control fixture accidentally populated minimap dots outside its synthetic map and independently invalidated the band; that failed to distinguish the hook and was corrected before the pixel-only negative control.

The private paired ARM experiment used source `b69083dc07ccd46150ee92e25109ee7b1e3e5235`, based on shared `e23038d3b99b2ccf536f89887ffebab845c340ad`. ON added only `SC64_HUD_BUFFER_REUSE`, `SC64_HUD_VRAM_BANDS`, and `SC64_MINIMAP_RASTER`; OFF used the prior Showcase flags. On one retained map-1 Showcase custom-game save with a Protoss human and three mixed AI opponents, all nine sampled state and framebuffer checkpoints matched over 900 updates. Mean HUD time was 112,896 OFF and 66,172 ON guest cycles; mean full-loop time was 865,290 and 761,046 cycles. Full-loop time includes presentation wait, and these figures describe this emulator workload rather than general gameplay, hardware performance or a 30 fps result.

At managed activation source `03c52f38ba87c73572544ca4294ee3a1bd027d66`, the actual normal Showcase Make recipe produced the same ON ROM SHA-256 (`74942fda05d05ec1e2e0e1f919bde83f702e82ffda8381272d3bfc710c78e8a4`) as the measured private ON experiment. BSS ended at `0203b0e0` and IWRAM at `03004a60`, within unchanged guards; the OFF experiment ended at `0203df40` and `03004a90`. Independent header, SRAM-marker, raw ELF payload and FF-padding checks passed. The 104 prepared input hashes matched before and after the build; private data is not committed.

The ASan/UBSan full-pixel HUD fixture passed with alternating poisoned pages, Showcase label transitions, selection and resource changes, open menu and cursor changes, messages, minimap visible/explored changes, pause/result and clear transitions. Eight focused Make flag/configuration tests passed, including invalid modes and rapid toggles. A clean unprepared original-art `./dev build` and `./dev test` passed at `03c52f3` (853 Python tests, 23 skipped, plus native checks); source-boundary and doctor passed. A fresh-process normal Showcase SRAM test passed after 9,000 updates and 900 resumed updates with three mixed AI opponents: saved/restored and reference/resumed fingerprints, including active production queues, agreed and SRAM was unchanged. This is not a complete transient-state comparison.

Commands, SHA-256 values, maps, both private ROMs and ELFs, per-update samples, negative-control logs, full-pixel fixture logs and SRAM receipts are retained in the ignored `.local/show-hud/` directories of `showcase-hud-cache-20261031` and `showcase-hud-activation-20261031`. No assets were generated or committed. P8/P9 and experimental Scarab products require independent qualification when their staged profiles are integrated.
