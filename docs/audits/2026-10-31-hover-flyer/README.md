# Hover integration and flyer pose sidecars

The hover integration preserves the original walk and facing source banks and their complete animations. Idle flying units use their source walk cycle when one exists; a flyer without a cycle retains its facing frame. The seven-ID packed-pose selection is 0, 37, 38, 41, 42, 43 and 65. Firebat (32) and other unselected units retain the original complete pixel-blit animation. This supersedes the six-ID selection in the shared-walk audit; it does not remove poses or change actor limits, gameplay state or cadence.

At source `41359a4`, a prepared-input ASan/UBSan pixel oracle compared 6,880 selected pose, heading and clipping cases to the pixel blitter. Six prepared-input engine fixture profiles (Terran, Zerg, P7, Showcase, Races and Unified) passed the hover and movement checks, including idle Mutalisk and Overlord cycles, Wraith missing-cycle fallback, and drawing without modifying unit state. Four canonical ARM products linked with their existing RAM guards: BSS/IWRAM ends were `020370c0/03004b70`, `02037134/03004c40`, `020382ec/03004a18` and `0203df40/03004e08`, respectively. Their text sizes were 30,208,708, 28,855,820, 29,290,056 and 28,945,716 bytes. Terran text increased 2,009,460 bytes relative to walk-only, below the 2 MiB budget by 87,692 bytes. The 104 prepared-input hashes and 115 source/input manifest hashes were unchanged over the ARM runs. The walk and facing bank SHA-256 values were `0b1172629a22c559322ff181d8b4551e01d7199328c3a675501dc170c9380415` and `ade3d8f7126dfd737eac3d62f58506ada32476efcbc89b6f4096b900b83faed2`.

A paired custom-main Zerg emulator replay compared hover with the earlier six-ID selection against hover with the seven-ID selection, across 100 timed calls. All 90 sampled actor, building, fog and frame checkpoints matched in each pair. For 24 idle, visible Mutalisks, unit drawing fell from 31,047,170 to 18,857,088 guest cycles (−39.26%) and the complete loop from 71,948,914 to 61,482,020 (−14.55%). For 24 idle, visible Overlords, unit drawing fell from 40,942,075 to 25,357,943 (−38.06%) and the loop from 78,604,975 to 67,671,601 (−13.91%). In the moving Firebat workload, 23–24 of 24 were moving and 11–24 were visible in sampled calls; unit drawing rose from 12,600,267 to 14,066,361 (+11.64%) and the loop from 67,958,980 to 68,514,411 (+0.82%). These are synthetic emulator workloads, not hardware frame-rate or general performance claims. The nonvisual checkpoints do not independently verify pixels; the clipping oracle addresses that separately.

Prepared source banks and generated sidecars are local and are not committed. No Actions were dispatched.

The default original-art ROM SHA-256 was `e612df7f0bd1417754e8665ca293bc53eedb194d55f93e2b996ea54ede1bd69c`. Focused sidecar producer/Make and pair tests also passed independently in that worktree.
