# Carrier parent and child validation short-circuit

The existing Carrier and Interceptor row validators resolved source binding and owner before rejecting dead rows and rows with unrelated engine types. The Carrier flight source tick invokes the parent validator for every unit slot; launch paths also scan slots through the child validator. This change returns false for a dead or nonmatching-type row immediately after the same bounds checks. Binding and owner resolution are pure reads, and valid Carrier and Interceptor rows still take the original validation path. There is no change to production cost, child capacity, source tick cadence, combat, save formats or slot allocation.

A private instrumentation run on the retained natural P8 save, with three Arbiters, one Observer and four Carriers, attributed instrumented mean 12,989 cycles/frame to Carrier training source ticks, 122,664 to Interceptor flight source ticks and 6,340 to per-frame flight movement. Carrier child counting averaged 3.17 calls and 17,499 cycles within those sections; slot-reservation calls were zero in this particular window. Parent validation averaged 210.69 calls/frame. Timing and counters add overhead, and these are scoped guest measurements, not hardware timings.

For actual normal P8 on the same 900-update save, mean full-loop cycles changed 1,777,208 → 1,732,890 and update cycles 1,234,272 → 1,210,881 compared with `c32893d`. Initial fingerprints and nine sampled screen/palette checkpoints matched. Full Unit, Building and mineral arrays, P7/P8 state, generic service clock and cloak due/initialized words matched on every one of 901 active frames. The P8 ROM passed header, ELF-payload and 32 MiB padding checks, with unchanged BSS `0203899c` and IWRAM `030049e0`. The retained full 90,428-call P8 win and current-writer fresh continuation are from predecessor runtime `348b3b2`; they are not a new full replay of this guard-only change.

An eager-reference validator differential passed under ASan/UBSan in P7 and P8 vectors for represented types −1…256, alive values 0/1/2/255, owner/raw-owner and raw-type sentinels, generic/P7/P8 and override contexts, and invalid bounds. The corrected independent detector-owner oracle is a separate test of current generic/P7/P8 mapping and detector semantics; it is not a Carrier-specific proof. Normal Terran, Zerg and Showcase vectors do not define `SC64_P7_CARRIER_TRAINING_ENABLED`, so they do not compile these changed functions.

The first concurrent build attempts hit filesystem ENOSPC; their logs and incomplete outputs were retained. A diagnostic link had completed but its failed objcopy left a zero-byte ROM; a locked Make retry incorrectly considered it current, so a separate owned diagnostic ROM was constructed from the verified ELF. The resulting diagnostic samples are not product artifacts. Subsequent actual candidate and fixture builds were serialized under the shared advisory lock. Performance remains workload-specific and does not establish near-30 FPS.

On the retained P7 custom three-AI save, the same normal-source comparison changed mean full-loop cycles from 1,074,402 to 1,054,739 and update cycles from 673,067 to 657,890. Initial fingerprints and nine sampled state/pixel checkpoints matched; full Unit and Building arrays and generic service clock/due/initialized words matched on every one of 901 active frames. This remains above the earlier service-disabled P7 full-loop mean and does not close the generic-service performance hold.

Carrier training and Interceptor flight cadence/save tests, cache cadence, P7/P8 shared services, and the corrected independent 2,000-world owner-mask oracle for each of P7 and P8 passed under ASan/UBSan. The 108 pinned input hashes were unchanged after qualification. The final driver receipt mistakenly reported a raw length of 3 due to a local variable shadowing error after its validation; an independent recheck produced the correct length and its own receipt. The original receipt remains preserved.
