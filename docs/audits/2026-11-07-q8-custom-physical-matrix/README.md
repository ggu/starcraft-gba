# Experimental Flash custom opening matrix

A physical-button mGBA run completed all 234 custom opening configurations: two maps, three human races, and every ordered selection of one, two, or three AI races. Each case started with an erased 128 KiB Flash image, played 120 neutral updates, saved through the guest pause menu, loaded the exported image in a fresh guest, and compared the saved and restored named world. A further 60-update uninterrupted and resumed continuation also matched. The screen field is excluded from cross-process named-world comparisons. Each fresh image had one checksum-valid committed 0x48 record and an erased legacy 32 KiB area.

These are early-opening results. They do not establish pixel equivalence, completed or active research, nonzero Q8 debt, combat, wins, performance, or hardware behavior. All saved Q8 debt and research level arrays were zero in this matrix. The feature remains experimental and normal builds remain unchanged.
