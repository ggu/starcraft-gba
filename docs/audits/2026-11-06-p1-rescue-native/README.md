# P1 rescue source investigation and native custom Pylon observation

This is a bounded source and emulator observation, not proof of N64 rescue rules, hardware behavior, or frame timing. The user subsequently identified the reported real-device scenario as Protoss custom, not campaign P1.

## P1 source and GBA fixture

The authenticated owned USA ROM SHA-256 `e7d21880c13953b45b8f8db6b2b6cfa02daa29d35b23799a967e96f21001b020` and pinned extractor SHA-256 `6a84761a9a8074fcc393527a7b17cea194084e8ab49176ff82fa3bef8e1b1dc2` yield map `008/01E`, CHK SHA-256 `612f61f194e4a45a24b483940c7ff47a9af8399511ca1ec63449ae7e8ef4100a`. UNIT contains no raw-64 Probe. Raw owner 7 has six Zealots and two Dragoons; raw owner 1 has three Dragoons, a Nexus, two Pylons, a Forge, Gateway and Assimilator; raw owner 2 has Fenix. OWNR marks raw 1 and 2 rescue passive, raw 7 human. Fenix is raw 77, distinct from ordinary Zealot 65.

The authenticated ROM unit table `00C/001` SHA-256 `54356d7585c66cd463346996444fd208336eaacf6335d30eb2688aa73c9cb2df` has supply-required values Probe 2, Zealot 4, Dragoon 4, Fenix 0, and supply-provided Nexus 18 and Pylon 16. These are half-supply units; the matching GBA rules are in `starcraft/race_rules.h`. Thus the six Zealots and five Dragoons total 44 half-units after the three Dragoons transfer; Fenix adds zero. A Nexus plus one Pylon supplies 34 half-units; both Pylons supply 50. In user-facing whole-supply terms these are 22/17 and 22/25 respectively. This arithmetic assumes the listed actors survive, the corresponding buildings are completed and transfer, and no other game state changes.

MRGN 6 is source rectangle (448,1696)–(1568,2400). P1's authored GBA rescue in `race_campaign_adapter.h` uses that rectangle at half scale for the ownership transfer. It includes the Nexus at (480,2224), one Pylon at (1056,1920), and the three owner-1 Dragoons; the other Pylon (256,2240) and Forge (240,2176) lie outside it. A source-only engine fixture observed 44/34 after that scripted rescue and could not queue a Probe until a fixture Pylon supplied additional cap. This is not an ordinary-input hardware observation.

The decoded P1 TRIG stream has no Give Units action and no Probe creation action. Its only CreateUnitWithProperties action targets raw-84 Observer in a presentation sequence; the elapsed-time setup sets Force 1 to 400 minerals and 100 gas, while another action sets Force 2 resources. This does not prove how the native engine's implicit rescue-passive mechanism transfers a force: it may depend on contact, player, other rules, or implementation details outside those actions. Pinned OpenBW `bwgame.h:8094–8095` and `:17201–17202` explicitly leave rescue-passive unimplemented, so it is not an oracle for transfer breadth. Do not infer the original game requires losses or that the GBA is wrong solely from the fixture. No native P1 rescue or supply was observed in the attempts below.
