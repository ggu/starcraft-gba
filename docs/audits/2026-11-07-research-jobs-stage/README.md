# Experimental producer research and journal

The current bounded-receipt branch derives settlement metadata from each validated active 0x45 row and its versioned quote. It remains experimental and is not enabled in normal ROMs; guarded ARM links and complete gameplay qualification are pending. The entries below record successive historical stages and their results; statements about earlier save refusal, receipt arrays or missing hooks describe those checkpoints, not the current branch.

## Historical stages

This is unfinished, default-off work and cannot be enabled in a ROM. The flag `SC64_EXPERIMENTAL_RESEARCH_JOBS` rejects non-HOST compilation and requires the existing experimental journal. It is connected to an experimental HOST-only custom producer menu and update tick, but not to combat. While the flag is enabled, both direct race save/load and their public wrappers refuse all operations before reading or writing SRAM; no test or native result is claimed here.

The prototype reads and writes the real `Building` fields for a completed Forge or Cybernetics Core. A custom compact owner and its wallet are supplied by the caller, which must authenticate owner activity, wallet binding, power, exact prerequisite, scenario maximum and the full duplicate-job scan. Admission validates all inputs before debiting. A job stages family in `level`, original payer in `queue[0]`, target level in `queue[1]`, and remaining duration in `queue_progress`, with an empty production queue. Every tick validates the supplied payer against both the producer's current owner and staged payer. Ambiguous ownership changes fail closed; there is not yet a transfer/death/cancel cleanup or refund path. The staged fields are not an authenticated save encoding and must not be written as an active job into any journal.

Legacy generic human weapon/armor levels are floors for both corresponding typed families, not additive bonuses. A legacy job pending in the same group blocks admission; prices and target use the effective floor. Other owners must not receive the human legacy floor or pending state. There is no automatic conversion or seeding of old saves, and generic pending jobs require reciprocal blocking by the future integrated menu. Shield and range upgrades receive no invented legacy floor. This remains an experimental policy, not a completed migration policy or combat integration.

Campaign binding is absent. Raw actor origin remains trigger provenance and cannot identify a current controller after rescue/capture. Future campaign support requires an authenticated current-controller and payer mapping against the relevant live or detached mission context; collapsed enemy owners must not be reverse-guessed. The staged caller-supplied validation is not evidence that those bindings, prerequisites, duplicate scans or wallet pointers are correct in actual gameplay.

Before any gameplay or ROM enablement: independently validate job fields for each building codec and a distinct tag; implement atomic save/load preflight and old-format coexistence; establish payer and lifecycle rules for cancellation, destruction, transfer, reset and power loss; wire building-context UI and all affected damage, armor, shields, range and specialized attacks; then qualify real ordinary-input flows, unchanged legacy saves, all products, bounded memory and timing. Full upgrade gameplay, source-policy fidelity and hardware behavior remain open.

The HOST stage hooks the shared destruction path before its other side effects. Death proceeds even if a staged receipt is malformed; that case credits nobody and leaves a sticky host fault that refuses saving/loading. A valid explicit cancel refunds the full quoted price; destruction credits three quarters, rounded down, saturating each wallet at its existing representation limit. Both resources and the job clear together, without signed overflow; repeated cancellation/destruction cannot pay twice. The three-quarter policy is pinned PC-source behavior and is not N64-verified. A dead slot retaining an unresolved staged job cannot be reused, and guarded building transfers reject a change of owner while such a job is marked. This deliberately conservative failure behavior is for the host experiment, not a claim about final player-facing behavior. Campaign jobs remain unsupported; some campaign-specific assignment paths are outside this staged custom proof.

On the 1286edbf source base, the constructed actual-P10 host fixture at `3eeb57d7` passed under the shared native lock. It exercised a staged real `Building` in the live array, host save/load refusal and unchanged SRAM, payer-bound full cancellation, three-quarter destruction refund, repeated settlement, wrong-owner and overflow rejection, guarded transfer and stale-slot reuse. A separate actual-P10 experimental-journal fixture, with the job-stage flag disabled, successfully wrote and loaded research records as a positive control. The 67 pinned input hashes were unchanged before and after; local logs and manifests are `.local/research-lifecycle/`. The transfer test exercises the guard directly rather than an ordinary control capture, and both are constructed HOST states. This does not qualify a native/N64 refund policy, gameplay, persistence for active jobs, or a release build.

The `fe72dd8f` HOST-only follow-up binds admission to an actual custom-game slot, active Protoss owner, owner wallet and producer power. It scans staged Forge/Core jobs for malformed receipts and same-owner/family duplicates, and checks same-owner completed Core, Archives or Fleet Beacon for level two/three where applicable without assuming those prerequisite buildings must themselves be powered. A volatile per-slot receipt records the original payer, family, target and price; settlement checks that receipt and refunds the original payment even if completed levels changed afterward. The receipt is never accepted from SRAM. Human wallets use signed-int bounds and AI banks use 65535. P10 HOST admission/lifecycle and separate job-stage-off journal writer positive control both passed with 67 verified inputs unchanged; logs are `.local/research-bound-admission/`. These are constructed states, not ordinary inputs or evidence of native refund behavior. The earlier human-wallet fixture failure at `f5fcdc74` (local `gas` shadowing the global) is retained in `.local/research-wallet/stage.log`; this follow-up corrected the fixture and reran it.

The HOST-only custom Forge/Core menu and update tick are staged behind the same non-ROM flag. They expose the three producer families and cancellation, refuse generic research admission when a matching typed job is active, and pause staged jobs when power is absent; completed prerequisites are checked at admission, not rechecked every tick. The tick authenticates the live receipt/owner and rejects malformed or duplicate marked jobs before advancing. The stage does not apply completed levels to combat: do not interpret its menu as usable upgrade gameplay. Save/load remain refused while the flag is on.

At `415a1307`, a fresh constructed P10 custom HOST run exercised B/A producer menu input, paid admission, reciprocal legacy-pending block, power-loss pause and staged completion, plus malformed raw producer identity rejection. The separate job-stage-off journal write/load control passed, and all 67 pinned inputs matched before and after. Receipts are `.local/research-menu-identity/`. This does not exercise the resulting level in combat or establish ordinary-input save/restore. The non-HOST compile prohibition and all-save/all-load refusal remain in force.

The P10 HOST policy correction at `81b72111` passed the same stage and job-stage-off journal positive control with the 67 pinned inputs unchanged (`.local/research-shield-policy/`). Level-one Plasma Shields is admitted without a Core; levels two and three require a completed same-owner Core. The earlier HOST test had over-restricted level one and is superseded. These are pinned PC-source prerequisite rules; no N64 timing or complete upgrade parity is claimed.

The next HOST-only stage wires ordinary stock-selected weapon IDs 13/14 to ground/air typed levels and ordinary unit HP armor IDs 5/6 to their typed levels. Human legacy values remain floors rather than additive bonuses; unknown/untypeable source IDs retain the existing generic behavior. The standard raw-66 Dragoon gets the pinned OpenBW +64 source-pixel Singularity increment (32 at this engine's half scale) in ordinary attack and acquisition ranges. Hero Dragoon handling, shields, buildings, special/precomputed attacks and several producer/projectile paths are not covered. These hooks must not be described as complete combat integration.

At `16f6ea2c`, the constructed actual-P10 HOST fixture passed selected-weapon family, human legacy-floor and owner-isolation checks, an ordinary stock HP armor hit, and raw-66 Dragoon range checks. The latter exercised `tick_combat` against both ground and air targets at a distance outside the base range and inside the staged upgraded range; it did not test a complete match or every acquisition path. The separate job-stage-off journal positive control passed at `9fc556f0`; runtime was unchanged by the following test-only commit. The 67 pinned inputs remained unchanged (`.local/research-combat-stage/`, `.local/research-range-combat/`). Shield mitigation remains untouched pending the independent Q8 policy and storage review.

A separate `aa0ceef8` P10 HOST check encoded and decoded a live staged producer through the actual typed building-row codec and matched the compact owner, job family, target and remaining duration. This is only a field bit-transport check. No tag has been allocated, the receipt is not encoded, and the public writer and loader still refuse the stage; no active-job persistence or detached preflight is established (`.local/research-row-roundtrip/`).

Constructed P10 and Z9 HOST checks at `8956ab05` exercised custom-game research wallet isolation for one, two and three bot slots (compact owners 1, 3 and 4), inactive and neutral rejection, cancellation, and the applicable typed or packed building-row bit transport. Both passed with 589 verified inputs unchanged; see `.local/research-custom-rosters/`. This is a bounded constructed-state check, not a completed skirmish, campaign qualification or active-job save/load proof.

A detached, read-only custom candidate audit at `e0a9aa3a` accepts explicit candidate rows, levels, player races and bot count rather than reading the live match. It rejects malformed job fields, mismatched payer/producer, duplicates, concurrent matching legacy work and nonzero typed levels for inactive or non-Protoss owners. P10 and Z9 constructed HOST fixtures passed with 589 inputs unchanged (`.local/research-detached-owners/`). The helper is not connected to any journal selector, does not reconstruct paid receipts, and does not prove historical payment, campaign identity or active-job persistence. A paid job may outlive its power or prerequisites, so their absence alone is not treated as malformed candidate state.

An independent pinned OpenBW review found that an admitted upgrade continues if its prerequisite building is later lost. The HOST experiment follows that PC-source ordering; the earlier host-only prerequisite-recheck behavior was corrected. Neither that ordering nor the existing power-loss pause is established as N64 behavior.

The volatile HOST paid-receipt array is diagnostic staging, approximately eight bytes per building. At 160 buildings this is about 1.28 KiB and exceeds the current Z9 static EWRAM reserve on its own. It is not a production memory design: a future GBA implementation needs an authenticated compact row/immutable-quote reconstruction or independently proven free storage, without reducing actors or impact capacity. Checksums establish record consistency, not cryptographic proof of historical payment.

At `c98542e2`, the separate `SC64_EXPERIMENTAL_RESEARCH_JOBS_JOURNAL` HOST-only experiment supersedes the earlier all-save refusal only for supported custom layouts: it writes a distinct 0x45 namespace, preflights detached rows and reconstructs receipts before publishing a selected record. The ordinary job-stage flag without this extra flag still refuses saves and loads, and non-HOST builds remain prohibited. Campaign saves remain unsupported. This is record consistency and authenticated interpretation of staged fields, not cryptographic proof that a historical payment occurred.

Constructed P10 producer and separate-process consumer checks passed for genuine current-main `1286edbf` 0x43 bytes, a genuine 0x44 predecessor, new 0x45 active jobs, and 0x43/0x45 and 0x44/0x45 mixed journals. The original 0x43 and 0x44 writers each made a real Gateway queue, destroyed it, and reused its slot as an idle Forge; stale queue bytes, including a dead Forge row, survived loading. New admission clears only the inert live row as part of its successful transaction. Two deliberately damaged copies of the mixed 0x43/0x45 journal tested fallback: a bad checksum and a checksum-valid invalid bot count in the newer record both selected the untouched 0x43 record. The 589 pinned inputs were unchanged in the guarded fallback run. Receipts and logs are `.local/research-job-journal-v2/` and `.local/research-job-journal-v3/`; these generated files are not committed. Earlier fixture failure from a missing prerequisite is retained in `.local/research-job-journal-initial/` and was corrected in the test setup, not the runtime validation.

These are constructed HOST states and synthetic corruption tests, not an ordinary-input playthrough or a ROM qualification. They do not establish complete combat/shield behavior, N64 research policy, all product layouts, or a production memory design. The volatile per-building receipt remains too large for the documented Z9 reserve; no release flag is enabled.

At combined runtime `8a96f995`, the bounded experiment replaces both the live per-building paid array and detached load-plan receipt array with a temporary receipt reconstructed from the active row and the immutable namespace-0x45 quote table. The table is version-specific; changing that wire interpretation requires a new version. This removes those 1.28 KiB arrays, but does not prove historical debit against deliberately forged valid state. Owner, raw producer, job fields, target, timer, duplicate jobs and applicable candidate context are checked; inert stale queue bytes remain distinct from an active job. Destruction still proceeds for malformed state without credit and records a sticky fault.

With 589 inputs unchanged, constructed P10 and Z9 HOST lifecycle tests, the P10 combat-routes sanitizer fixture, and the P10 active-job journal build passed. Genuine 0x43 and 0x44 predecessor records passed fresh-process load, mixed new-0x45 write/load and both checksum-damaged and checksum-valid semantically invalid newer-record fallback cases. See `.local/research-bounded-v5/` for local receipts. Six ARM links passed with `SC64_EXPERIMENTAL_RESEARCH_JOURNAL`, `SC64_EXPERIMENTAL_RESEARCH_JOBS`, `SC64_EXPERIMENTAL_RESEARCH_JOBS_JOURNAL` and `SC64_RESEARCH_BOUNDED_ARM_PROBE` enabled, retaining normal linker guards. Z9 and Z10 each have 732 bytes EWRAM and 1,936 bytes IWRAM headroom to their linker guard; Z7 has 2,900 and 1,024 bytes respectively. Logs, exact inputs and ELF/map hashes are `.local/research-bounded-arm/`. These margins do not measure stack high-water, runtime behavior or hardware performance. The feature remains off in normal products; complete shields and specialized combat, ordinary gameplay, persistence on hardware and all-campaign qualification remain open.
