# P10 route-tail attribution

Experimental instrumentation only; no production flag or route policy changed. The ordinary-input P7 tape was replayed using the delivered P10 package inputs and a fresh physical mGBA process. The uninstrumented guarded control ROM SHA-256 was `ff94a2ea9f98404c97f44a8b6b7bf557b3572c761de530d867032eeb8f76b10b`; the instrumented ROM was `9c878696accd7c73e661013744c5fb30079e698b516638f5990969b4d611ff55`. Input manifests before and after matched. The retained local receipts are `.local/qualification/movement-v2/` and the preceding phase audit describes the tape and package provenance.

Across tape ticks 8020–9019, there were 958 advancing and 42 nonadvancing calls, with three prior modal acknowledgments. Eleven selected state samples, full Unit/building/P7-state arrays, fog, displayed indexed framebuffer and palette fingerprints matched; the screen, mode and frame timeline matched on every call. This is sampled equivalence, not a full-state or every-frame proof. The control mean was 2,253,117 cycles per advancing call and the instrumented mean 2,312,076 (+2.617%); timings include instrumentation and overlapping scopes. Guarded BSS/IWRAM ends were `020391e0/03004b20` and `020393c0/03004950` respectively; stack high-water was not measured. No physical hardware frame-rate claim follows.

A 16-entry diagnostic ring was drained on every call. The reader rejected ring overflow or disagreement between its event count and the build counter. Five field-build events were observed:

- Tick 8143: `race_resource_depot_reachable` requested key 32774 in slot 13 four times. The first build began with an invalid slot; all four results remained partial. The following three rebuilt the same partial key, taking 57,976, 62,748 and 75,294 cycles; the first took 24,732 cycles.
- Peak tick 8347: `move_toward` requested point key 10270 in invalid slot 12 and produced a complete field in 2,235,856 instrumented cycles. The same call's movement scope was 2,637,146 cycles and orders scope 3,007,005 cycles. These scopes overlap and cannot be added.

All five events recorded invalidation epoch 115. The last recorded `invalidate_path_grid` caller resolved to `spawn_build`; the tracer did not timestamp that invalidation and does not establish that it immediately caused either miss. Invalid slot and repeated-partial reasons describe observed slot state, not a complete history of why it became invalid. The partial rebuilds identify a possible redundant-work investigation, but their safety and representative value remain unproven. The peak complete flood is the larger measured target. This tracer records field builds, not route hits, monotone witness work, all invalidation history, or all pathfinding costs. No candidate optimization, changed capacity, or gameplay cadence is enabled by this audit.

## Origin and frontier follow-up

A separately guarded diagnostic replay (`66e15528bd6047b7604288cb9e58599a16a0ea155cfd36753fd1eef20e6634b1`) added source and flood counters. It again matched the selected state and full displayed framebuffer/palette samples, complete screen/mode/frame timeline, counts and frozen inputs; mean intrusive overhead was 2.617%. Its local receipts are `.local/qualification/movement-v3/`. The peak point query ran from cell 1044 (20,8) to 10270 (30,80), Manhattan distance 82 on the 128-column map. It expanded and visited 12,001 cells without reaching the source. The P10 vector does not enable `SC64_ROUTE_PARTIAL_FLOOD`: the point flood unconditionally exhausts its component, and for this specific unreachable origin, an origin-limited flood would still exhaust that component.

The four same-key depot partials visited 15, 91, 123 and 155 cells and expanded 0, 63, 91 and 123 respectively; all four origins were reached. A resumable frontier could avoid repeated prefixes only if it preserves exact FIFO and visited semantics, reestablishes borrowed walkability marks, and validates ownership, key, slot and topology. The queue is shared with point and depot floods, legacy BFS and monotone-witness scratch, and is unioned with save/decode and impact scratch in applicable vectors. Route reset/invalidation and slot eviction or repurposing must also invalidate any ownership. This inventory rules out assuming that consecutive same-key calls alone retain a usable frontier. No frontier reuse is implemented or qualified here.

## Immutable terrain connectivity

The P7 terrain in the exact pinned control ROM contains a single 12,723-cell goal component that includes the peak source. Both cells and their four cardinal neighbors are base-walkable; their base-terrain shortest path is 210 tiles. The 16,384-byte blocked-map slice at ROM offset `0x1b4c5ec` has SHA-256 `e067cce13f78899a185006aad500d9846dccf682cc1dd36e78679fcaf810da5f`. `tools/qualification/sc64_p10_base_component.py` checks both the full pinned ROM hash and slice hash before reproducing this BFS. The dynamic field's failure to reach the source cannot be rejected by an immutable-base connectivity prefilter. `build_path_grid` starts from retained terrain and applies live building/door occupancy, so the exact dynamic reason requires a separate grid inspection; it is not established by the static map alone.
