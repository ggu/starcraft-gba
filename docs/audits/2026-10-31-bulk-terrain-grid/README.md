# Bulk terrain-grid initialization experiment

The optional `SC64_BULK_TERRAIN_GRID` path packs the low blocked bit of existing imported map bytes eight cells at a time into the existing `path_walkable` grid, before the unchanged building and scenario-door overlays. It retains the scalar path for the procedural skirmish arena, Showcase scene and configurations without an eligible imported packed grid. No new asset, persistent state or BSS is required. The path is disabled by default.

At source `626b493`, an ASan/UBSan differential test passed 374 randomized lengths and arbitrary source bytes, including odd cell counts, preserved tail bits and guard bytes. Prepared-input full-engine fixtures compared complete scalar and bulk rebuilt grids across 36, 24, 27 and 30 map/overlay cases for Terran, Zerg, Protoss and Showcase profiles. A Terran fixture also compared 14 actual T4 doors open and closed and rebuilt the grid after an actual save/load. A first version of that fixture hit an older staged-only catalog assertion; the canonical fixture starts the live campaign directly and the failed log is retained locally.

Two same-source, flag-off/on Zerg ARM products loaded the identical three-AI SRAM at frame 9000 and ran through frame 9900. Each update remained in active gameplay; all nine sampled semantic-state, owner-population and framebuffer/palette hashes matched, including the independently verified saved and resumed fingerprints. Average full-loop guest cycles fell from 654,951 to 643,403 (−1.76%); update from 325,835 to 315,603 (−3.14%); and orders from 234,244 to 224,009 (−4.37%). Full-loop p95 fell from 1,104,035 to 842,370, p99 from 1,664,425 to 1,404,105, and maximum from 2,246,356 to 1,965,370. On frame 9214, whose scalar diagnostic included a grid rebuild, order time fell from 1,822,204 to 1,112,582 cycles. Separate route-only spikes remained. This is one synthetic emulator continuation and does not establish physical GBA or general frame rate.

All four normal ARM links passed the existing RAM guards: Terran `020370c0/03004b70`, Zerg `02037134/03004c40`, Protoss `020382ec/03004a18` and Showcase `0203df40/03004e08` (BSS/IWRAM endpoints). The first Showcase attempt ran out of disk space; its failure log is retained and the Showcase-only retry passed. The 104 prepared-input hashes remained unchanged. The experiment was enabled explicitly in fresh builds; a mode switch requires rebuilding because the current Make rules do not track this experimental compiler flag. No Actions were dispatched and no generated data, ROM or private asset was committed.
