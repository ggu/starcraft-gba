# Canonical fresh route-cell writer qualification

The packed route writer is enabled in the four canonical products. It clears direction bits from an all-unreachable field only when a cell is first discovered. The generic writer remains responsible for seed cells. Depot rebuilds clear the field before discovery, including re-created bounded fields; borrowed walkability bits are restored. The documented `CANONICAL_ROUTE_INDEX=0` opt-out also disables this writer by default. Setting `CANONICAL_ROUTE_NEW_CELL=1` explicitly while the route index is disabled is rejected.

Qualification used source `751c6070c4a6d925d8b4c455f1dccfd8ea4c7d7e`, a merge of shared `652ff63bffec785dbead6f24fa22716c0a47e0f2` and route candidate `cfef9543eb51741793b77526d34fa6ae1c578ff7`. Its successor `7e3132811554a9ade569bf38b001109fa4c1d8b1` changes only the Make default and its tests; all four default compiler flag vectors match with the same audio selection. Private inputs were not committed. The 104 prepared input hashes matched before and after the four ON links and the Showcase OFF link.

- The clean original-art build and suite passed at both `751c607` and the Make-only successor `7e31328` (852 Python tests, 23 skipped); source-boundary and doctor checks also passed at the successor. At `751c607`, the full-field route BFS oracle and bounded, unbounded and active-stride depot BFS, first-step, restoration, boundary and canary checks passed. The managed-option tests cover vectors, invalid values and same-tick configuration switches.
- Actual normal Terran, Zerg Z6, Protoss P7 and Showcase ON links passed their existing guards. BSS/IWRAM endpoints respectively were `020370c0/03004b40`, `02037134/03004c10`, `020382ec/030049e0`, and `0203df40/03004a90`. Showcase OFF was `0203df40/03004e08`. Showcase ON and OFF passed independent ROM header, SRAM marker, payload and padding checks.
- Fresh-process SRAM qualification of all four normal ON ROMs at `751c607` passed on map 1 with three mixed AI opponents, after 9,000 updates plus 900 resumed updates. Saved-in-pause, restored fingerprints including active queues, reference continuation and unchanged SRAM all passed at frame 9901. Results are retained under root `.local/qualification/20261031-newcell-shared-sram/`.
- On a retained Showcase three-AI save, 900 updates of OFF and ON had identical state and pixel hashes at nine checkpoints, identical 117 route builds and 2,700 hits, and the same final saved-state reference. Mean full-loop guest cycles were 864,978 OFF and 865,290 ON (+0.036%); mean orders were 258,505 and 258,591 (+0.033%). This does not establish a Showcase speedup.
- On the earlier Zerg three-AI save, 900 updates had matching sampled state and pixels. Mean full-loop cycles were 735,522 OFF and 733,961 ON (about -0.21%). An artificial depot-heavy stress run that forced one full depot rebuild each update measured mean depot-call cycles of 1,070,033 OFF and 1,010,112 ON (about -5.6%). It is not an ordinary-gameplay or hardware frame-rate measurement.

Retained local evidence includes commands, logs, source/input hashes, maps, ROMs, ELFs and per-update samples in the ignored `.local/activation-qualification/` directories of the `route-newcell-combined-20261031` and `route-newcell-activate-20261031` worktrees, and `.local/depot-profile/` in `route-newcell-depot-20261031`. No general 30 fps claim follows from these measurements. P8/P9 staged products are not normal product vectors in this shared tree and require separate qualification when integrated.
